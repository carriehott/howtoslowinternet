🌀 how to slow internet 🌀

The how to slow internet name is a reference to truncated search engine syntax and how technology has the ability to change our language, and in turn our expectations, our sense of time, and our sense of agency.

The how to slow internet project is a search for a communication network that is experimental, physical, and interpersonal.

The how to slow internet project's intention is to be slow, weird, and human.

how to slow internet is stewarded by Carrie Hott, Andrea Steves, and Timothy Furstnau. The network includes artists, designers, archivists, writers, and researchers on three continents.
